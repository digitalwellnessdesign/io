Step 2

Choose to finish with a redesigned ritual + phone

We'll shape your ritual

Together we'll design a habit or routine that fits your real life and moves you forward.

  • No-scroll morning grounding — start your day without spiraling into feeds
  • Wind-down that works — a cue that helps your nervous system actually rest
  • Weekly check-in — for groceries, finances, or other planning
  • Protected creative blocks — focused time for writing, art, or flow work
  • Gentle movement nudges — that fit your real energy, not forced

Then redesign your iPhone to support it

We'll apply 3–5 intentional changes to reduce noise and reinforce your new rhythm. Depending on your goals, I'll guide you through options like:

  • Focus Modes that shift with your time, place, or energy
  • Home screen layouts that center what matters and hide what doesn't
  • Automations to create seamless starts, stops, or transitions
  • Shortcuts for routines, boundaries, or intention resets
  • Widgets that gently guide your attention where you want it
  • Quiet cues like filtered notifications and hidden apps to protect your space
